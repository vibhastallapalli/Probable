CREDIBILITY FIX: MAKE THE SCORE SHOW ITS REASONING (planned, code later)
=====================================================
The problem this solves: if a judge asks why should we trust the AI score, right now the honest
answer is a small model looked at 8 frames and gave a number. Too soft. This is the fix.

The reframe (say this out loud in the pitch): we are NOT claiming the AI is objectively right.
Coolness is subjective, that is the point. We claim it is impartial, consistent, and transparent,
applying FIFA's own Puskas Award criteria [the real recognised standard for judging goal quality].
That is what no human judge or sportsbook can offer for a subjective bet.

The one build ask:
[ ] Add one plain English sentence per rubric category to the model output, saying WHY that
    category got its score
    example: difficulty 8 of 10, struck first time from outside the box under pressure
    nearly free since the model already reasons to produce the number, we just surface it in the
    JSON schema we already use
[ ] Surface those sentences in the UI next to each category score so the judge SEES the AI justify
    itself against a named rubric instead of pulling a number from nowhere
[ ] First step before any edit: read scoring.js and the output schema and report the current
    output shape, where the rubric categories live, how output flows to the UI, and whether the
    schema is strict, BEFORE changing anything

Also lean on the rubric out loud: we did not invent what makes a goal good, we encoded the criteria
FIFA uses for its own goal of the year award.
